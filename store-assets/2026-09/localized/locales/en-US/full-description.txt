Pizza tonight? One more episode? Take a walk? Turn small everyday choices into a quick dice roll.

Yes/No Randomizer gives you a random yes or no with a playful 3D animation. Type a question, or leave it blank, then tap Decide! Roll again whenever you want another random result.

Keep your answer close while you multitask. On devices that support picture-in-picture, tap Float answer after a roll to show your answer in a floating window over another app. Tap the window to access New Answer and get another answer without returning to the full screen.

Enjoy light and dark themes that follow your device settings. Choose from 18 languages inside the app. Your question and answer are preserved when you rotate your device, change languages, or switch themes.

No account, no ads, and no internet connection required. Open the app, ask a question if you like, and let chance choose.

Great for casual choices, friendly challenges, and those little should-I moments. Every result is random: a fun nudge for everyday decisions.
