PRIVACY POLICY — YES/NO RANDOMIZER

REVIEW DRAFT — 23 November 2026
This proposed policy has not replaced the published policy. Before publication,
confirm the app's declared age groups and support-data practices, and resolve
Google Play's email-address finding. See docs/data-safety-review.txt for the
review status. The sections below describe the reviewed app and a proposed
support-data retention policy; they do not certify Play policy compliance.

Developer and contact

Yes/No Randomizer is provided by Sergey Blashkov. For privacy questions or
requests about information you have sent to the developer, contact
sblashkov@gmail.com.

Questions, answers and local settings

The app lets you enter a question and obtain a yes/no answer. The current
question, its original or translated text, and the answer are held in the app
and Android's saved state so the current interaction can survive supported
screen, language and lifecycle changes. The app does not maintain a permanent
question history or send questions or answers to a developer-operated server.

Language preferences, translation preferences and model-download choices are
stored locally. Downloaded translation models are also stored on the device.
The app has no account-registration or sign-in feature and no email-address
entry field. It does not display advertisements.

On-device language identification and translation

The app uses Google's ML Kit language-identification and translation SDKs.
According to Google's ML Kit privacy documentation, input text and translation
results are processed on the device and are not sent to Google servers by those
APIs. This also applies when a question contains mixed languages or personal
information.

Core decisions work offline. Translation may first require language-model
downloads. The app asks before downloading missing models and requires Wi-Fi
for those downloads. Once the necessary models are available, the translation
itself can run offline.

Google SDK technical data

On-device translation does not mean the SDKs make no network requests or
collect no data. Google documents collection of device/OS and app/version
information, installation identifiers, performance and error metrics, API
configuration and input/output sizes, SDK events, and detected/source/target
languages. These metrics support diagnostics, usage analysis, maintenance and
improvement. ML Kit also describes use of Firebase Remote Config and Firebase
Installations for translation diagnostics. Documented metric traffic uses HTTPS.

When network requests occur, the receiving service can also see the connection's
IP address. Google's processing is subject to its applicable terms and privacy
policy. The app does not implement a separate developer-operated telemetry
endpoint.

Controls and Android services

The translation setting controls the app's translation behaviour. Language
identification may still run to determine whether to offer the Translate action.
The app does not provide a separate SDK-telemetry opt-out. Agreeing to or
declining a language-model download is not a general SDK-telemetry control.

Android backup is enabled for eligible app data. Depending on your device and
backup settings, Android may back up and restore eligible data. The developer
does not receive those backups. Your keyboard, Android backup and external
browser are separate services governed by their own settings and policies.

Retention and deletion

Current question and answer state is retained for restoring the current
interaction; its lifetime depends on Android's management of the app and task.
Preferences and models remain locally until removed through app/device storage
controls, app removal or system management. Android backup may restore eligible
data during a later installation.

Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it removes its local data, subject to
Android's backup/restore behaviour. Uninstalling stops future transmissions by
the app but does not itself delete data already received by Google. Google-held
SDK data is retained under Google's applicable policies; the developer cannot
directly view or erase that data through the app.

Contacting support

If you email the developer, your email address, message and any information you
choose to include are used to respond to your request. This is separate from the
app's question field. Support correspondence is not used for marketing.

Proposed support-retention practice, to be confirmed before publication:
support correspondence is kept only as long as needed to handle the request
and meet applicable legal obligations. You can request deletion of support
correspondence by emailing sblashkov@gmail.com. Requests are subject to any
applicable legal retention requirements. This does not promise deletion of
Google-held SDK metrics.

Children and families

The app has no advertising, accounts or public social features. The SDK data
processing described above can still apply when a child uses the app. Parents
and guardians can contact the developer about privacy questions or information
they have provided in support correspondence. The final audience wording and
any applicable consent requirements must be reviewed against the age groups
declared in Play Console before this draft is published.

Changes

The developer will publish an updated policy when these practices change and
provide any additional notices or choices required for the change. Publication
of an updated policy is not a substitute for any consent that is required.
